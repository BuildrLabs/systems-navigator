# Playbook: Con Edison shutoff notice or no power (New York City)
verified_on: 2026-10-16 (partial; see VERIFY section)

## Situation
User received a past-due or termination notice from Con Edison, had electric or gas shut off, or wants service turned back on.

## Identify the situation
| Situation | Urgency |
|---|---|
| Past-due bill, no termination notice yet | Medium - set up a payment arrangement now |
| Final notice of termination with a date | High - act before the date shown |
| Service already shut off | High - restoration steps below |
| Someone in the home depends on power for a medical reason | Emergency - medical certification pathway |
| Notice addressed to the building owner/landlord, tenants affected | High - tenant notice branch |
| Smell of gas, sparks, downed lines | Emergency - leave the area, call 911 and Con Edison's emergency line (look up in directory) |

## Key facts the assistant may state (sourced)
- NY Public Service Law section 32 sets minimum rules for terminations: a final notice must be sent at least 15 days before the termination date shown, and not until at least 20 days after payment was due. The notice must say why service may end, how to avoid it, and that complaint procedures exist.
- The same law requires protection from termination (or restoration of service) for residents who would face serious health or safety impairments, with written certification by a medical doctor, nurse practitioner or local board of health that termination or failure to restore would aggravate an existing medical emergency. Telephone certification may be allowed initially if the written certification follows within five business days.
- Con Edison's residential rights brochure says it will negotiate in good faith to set up payment agreements, lists special protections for elderly, blind and disabled customers, and tells customers they can complain to the NY Public Service Commission (PSC) at dps.ny.gov/complaints or 1-800-342-3377. [dir:nys-psc-consumer]
- Customers can ask Con Edison to set up a payment agreement; it is worth asking about assistance programs when calling. [dir:coned-customer-service]

## Steps: notice received, still on
1. Photograph the notice. Note the account number, amount, "pay by" date and termination date.
2. Call Con Edison today. Say: you got a termination notice and want to set up a payment agreement. Ask: what is the exact amount to stop the shutoff? what is the minimum payment today? are there assistance programs? Write down the representative's name and a reference number.
3. If anyone in the home has a medical need that depends on power, say so on the call and ask about the medical emergency process; then contact the doctor/NP/clinic for the certification.
4. If the account is in a landlord's name and you are a tenant, tell Con Edison you are a tenant and ask about tenant protections for the building. Also tell your landlord in writing.
5. Check ACCESS NYC for energy or other bill help. [dir:accessnyc]
6. If Con Edison refuses a reasonable arrangement or you think the notice is wrong, file a complaint with the PSC. [dir:nys-psc-consumer]
7. Put the termination date in the calendar with reminders.

## Steps: service already off (restore the lights)
1. Call Con Edison; ask: why was service shut off, the exact amount needed to restore, whether a payment agreement or partial payment can restore service, whether a deposit will be required, and how long restoration takes after payment.
2. Make the payment in a way that creates a receipt (online confirmation or reference number) and ask for the restoration order/reference number.
3. If someone has a medical emergency tied to power, say so first and ask for expedited restoration; ask the clinician for certification.
4. If it is cold weather and the home has no heat, call 311 and the PSC as well; if anyone is in danger, call 911.
5. If Con Edison does not restore after you meet the stated requirement, or you believe the shutoff was improper, file a PSC complaint and contact free legal help (Legal Aid; see directory) to ask about emergency court action.
6. Keep food/medication safety in mind: use a cooler, call the pharmacist about refrigerated meds.

## Call script
"Hello, my name is [name], account number [number], at [address]. I got a [termination notice / my service was shut off] on [date]. I want to set up a payment agreement and find out the exact amount needed to [stop the shutoff / restore service today]. [If applicable: someone in my home needs power for a medical reason.] Can I get your name and a reference number for this call?"

## What to bring/have ready
Latest bill, account number, ID, proof of income if applying for assistance, doctor/NP contact for certification, any letter from the landlord.

## The app must not say
- Specific shutoff-time or season rules (see VERIFY) unless confirmed
- That a shutoff was illegal or that Con Edison "must" restore by a given time
- Any number or address not returned by the directory tool
